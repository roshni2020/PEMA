Your rooms each room has its own scanned map

The active room is what the dashboard shows and what "where is the door" refers to.

Scan the active room

Option A · live: open the dashboard, press "Scan room", and slowly turn the laptop camera around once (30 s).
Option B · video: record a slow 20 to 60 second pan of the room with your phone, then drop the file here. Frames are sampled once a second and sent to the board; nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

What the scan does

Objects: YOLOX on the Arduino's Qualcomm Dragonwing chip, every frame.
Fixtures (door, window, table, bed, sofa, fridge, stove, sink, tv): the vision model on the Hexagon NPU, every third frame.
Placement: a slow pan is treated as a sweep, so time gives direction, box size gives rough distance. Approximate 3D, not a metric reconstruction.
