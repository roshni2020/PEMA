PEMA Personal Environmental Memory Agent

A private memory of your room, for people who can't see it well.

PEMA is built for low-vision and memory-impaired users. It runs on an Arduino with Qualcomm silicon on your desk, remembers where your things are, notices what moved while you were away, and can act on real devices, verifying every action. Nothing leaves your home.

1 You enroll once: a name and a private passphrase. No face data.
2 You scan each room once, by turning the camera around or uploading a short video.
3 PEMA remembers where objects are and notices when they move.
4 Ask "where are my keys" or "where is the door". It answers out loud.
5 It can turn the light on, and proves it did.
Privacy, plainly
  • Camera frames are analysed on the device and discarded. Only object names and positions are stored, on the board, never in a cloud.
  • It recognises objects, not faces. It verifies you by passphrase, never by biometrics. It cannot identify anyone else.
  • The camera has an off switch in the app, and the board's LED shows when it is watching.
  • The reasoning model runs on a local Qualcomm NPU. No internet is needed after setup.

Sign in or register on this board

Registering stores the name and passphrase in a file on the Arduino. Signing in with the same passphrase later verifies you for the session.
